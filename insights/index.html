Preparing for a Family Law Consultation

Bring relevant dates, existing orders, correspondence and details of any current arrangements. A focused initial discussion helps identify the legal issues and practical next steps.

Explain the family relationships and your current concerns. Bring relevant records such as existing orders, correspondence and financial details where applicable.

An attorney can clarify the issues, legal procedures and what further information may be required. General online guidance is not a substitute for individual advice.

Understanding Deceased Estate Administration

When someone dies, the estate administration process may involve reporting to the Master, collecting records, identifying assets and liabilities, and complying with statutory procedures.

Reporting, obtaining authority to administer the estate and preparing inventories may all be relevant depending on the estate and the stage reached.

Keep wills, identity documents and other estate papers organised, and ask the attorney which steps are appropriate for the individual circumstances.

Questions to Ask Before a Property Transfer

Before buying or selling, review the signed sale agreement, finance requirements, transfer costs, outstanding rates and any special conditions. Seek advice about the particular transaction.

Transaction documents may contain deadlines and conditions that affect the buyer and seller. The process can also involve financial institutions, the municipality and Deeds Office procedures.

Confirm fees, timelines, responsibilities and outstanding conditions with your attorney before making decisions or transferring funds.
