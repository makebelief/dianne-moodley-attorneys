WELCOME TO THE FIRM

Dianne Moodley Attorneys Inc.

Established in 1999. Committed to attentive legal service.

Dianne Moodley Attorneys Inc. is a legal practice based in Westcliff, Chatsworth, and serving clients in Durban and beyond. The firm describes a long-standing commitment to practical, attentive legal support.

The practice publicly lists family and divorce law, administration of deceased estates, High Court litigation, property transfers and business formation among its services. Attorney Dianne Moodley leads the practice.
